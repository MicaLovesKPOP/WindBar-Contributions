# WindBar Contributions

WindBar Contributions is the public creator hub for people who want to build, share, document, or propose content for WindBar.

WindBar itself follows a curated model: community creations are not automatically shown, endorsed, or distributed inside WindBar. Creators may publish their work independently, and exceptional work may be reviewed for possible official integration.

## What creators may eventually build

- Layout Profiles
- Visual Themes
- Color Schemes
- Start layouts/content
- Experience Presets
- Plugins that provide Modules, widgets, flyouts, settings, or integrations

Not every content type above has a finalized public format yet. Draft/future areas are clearly marked as such.

## Important status

WindBar is still working toward its first public release. Public schemas and plugin APIs are **not finalized**. Do not build long-term compatibility assumptions from placeholder files or examples in this repository until a format is explicitly marked stable.

## Community vs official

Community work remains owned and maintained by its creator unless a separate adoption arrangement is made.

Popularity may help a creation get noticed, but popularity alone does not make something official. Official WindBar content is content the WindBar project has deliberately reviewed, integrated/distributed, and agreed to maintain to WindBar's release standards.
