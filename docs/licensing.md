# Licensing and Asset Provenance

Every contribution considered for official integration must have clear licensing.

Creators should document:

- The license for their code/content
- The source and license of third-party assets
- Whether attribution is required
- Whether modification and redistribution are permitted

## Historical Windows-inspired content

Do not assume that an asset is freely redistributable because it shipped with an older Windows version.

For historical themes and recreations, prefer:

- Original/recreated assets
- Assets with a clear compatible license
- Lawful runtime use of system-provided resources where appropriate

WindBar may require assets to be replaced or recreated before official adoption if redistribution rights are unclear.

This document is project guidance, not legal advice.
