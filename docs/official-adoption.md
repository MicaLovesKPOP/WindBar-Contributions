# How Official Adoption Works

WindBar uses a curated contribution model.

Community creations are external by default. A creation may be shared, used, and maintained by its creator without ever becoming part of WindBar itself.

A typical path toward official adoption is:

1. The creator builds and shares the work externally.
2. Users test it and provide feedback.
3. The creator or community submits it for consideration.
4. WindBar reviews design quality, usefulness, architecture fit, performance, accessibility where applicable, security, licensing, and maintainability.
5. WindBar may request changes or adaptation to project standards.
6. If both sides agree, licensing/maintenance/credit arrangements are settled explicitly.
7. The creation is integrated or distributed as official WindBar content and enters WindBar's normal compatibility/release expectations.

## Popularity

Popularity and community demand are useful signals, but they do not guarantee adoption.

## Credit

Official adoption should preserve clear creator attribution where a community creator originated the work.

## Maintenance

The most important distinction is responsibility: once WindBar calls something official, the project accepts a meaningful maintenance and compatibility obligation for it.
