# Quality Guidelines

WindBar may consider exceptional community work for official integration. The bar for official adoption is intentionally higher than the bar for sharing something with other enthusiasts.

We consider:

- Correctness and stability
- Usefulness and clarity of purpose
- Visual quality and consistency
- Performance and resource use
- Accessibility where applicable
- Compatibility with WindBar architecture
- Security and truthful capability declarations
- Licensing and asset provenance
- Maintainability
- Testability
- Creator responsiveness/maintenance history where relevant
- Popularity and community interest as a signal, not as an automatic decision
- The long-term support burden the WindBar project would accept by adopting it

## Official does not mean popular

Popularity can make a creation more likely to be reviewed, but official content is content WindBar deliberately chooses to support and maintain.

## Historical themes and assets

Do not submit copyrighted assets simply extracted from Windows or another product unless you have the rights to redistribute them. Historical recreations should prefer original/recreated assets, lawful runtime references, or assets with a clear compatible license.
